# Narrative and execution

Keep vertical chronological alignment: time/mode at left, story card in the middle, execution frames further right. Row height is not duration. Story cards show simplified black-line director sketches with necessary location, purpose, action, camera, dialogue and post notes directly below.

Before approving a planning revision, compare it to the current human intent. Concrete failure examples: a lightly sleeping owner suddenly sitting up; a lamp itself illuminating the room when it should control other fixtures; thought overlays implying completed actions before they occur. Check capability assertions against attributable facts; explicitly fictional demonstration remains creative intent, not product verification.

Canvas may begin with sketches. Each story beat can contain several video tasks. Choose first/intermediate/end states only where they change action, composition or environment; maximum 10 per task, no minimum quota. Hard cuts are not morphs. A generated end-state still is not an observed video tail.

Do not replace the adopted/schematic execution set merely because photoreal files exist. Candidate groups carry exact image hashes; approval applies only to that group. Preserve narrative order separately from reference lineage. Time mode, story clock, suggested generation duration and edit intent remain separate fields.

Validate the current story and Canvas together after corrections, including adjacent beats. Do not regenerate unrelated approved work to standardize it.
